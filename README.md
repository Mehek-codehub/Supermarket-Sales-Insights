# Supermarket Sales Insights Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze supermarket sales performance and generate meaningful business insights. The project explores product category contributions, outlet performance, sales relationships, and statistical patterns to support data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

### 1. Overall Sales Performance
- Analyze total sales revenue.
- Calculate average sales value per record.
- Understand average product visibility.

### 2. Product Category and Sales Comparison
- Compare the sales contribution of the top four product categories against the remaining categories.
- Identify the highest- and lowest-performing product categories.
- Compare total sales across different outlet types.

### 3. Outlet Performance and Sales Relationships
- Compare Low Fat and Regular product sales across outlets.
- Analyze the relationship between product visibility and sales.
- Evaluate category sales contributions across outlet types.

### 4. Statistical Analysis of Sales
- Calculate average, median, minimum, and maximum sales.
- Analyze sales variation.
- Identify unusually high and low sales records.
- Compare average sales across product categories, outlet types, and outlet locations.

## Tools and Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculated columns
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive KPI cards
- Product category sales analysis
- Outlet performance comparisons
- Sales distribution and variation analysis
- Statistical measures
- High and low sales record identification

## Dataset
The project uses the BlinkIT Grocery dataset containing product, outlet, sales, visibility, and rating information.

## Expected Outcome
An interactive dashboard that helps understand supermarket sales performance, compare product categories and outlet operations, and explore statistical sales patterns.
